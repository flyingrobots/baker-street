## 1. Background Context

*The Adventure of the Misaddressed Receipt*

as one we had returned the previous Thursday.

“The contents are not. We must examine both before we accept the delivery.”

## 2. Problem Description

This fictional admission command checks the revision named on a receipt but
does not compare the enclosed paper with its recorded digest. A reader may
therefore accept evidence belonging to another revision.

## 2b. Proposed Solution

Compare the paper's calculated digest with the receipt before admission.
Refuse a disagreement without altering the existing case.

## 2c. Alternatives considered and rejected

Examine the filename alone: the wrong paper can bear the expected name.

## 2d. Acceptance Criteria

- A matching paper is admitted.
- A mismatched paper is refused before the existing case is altered.
- The refusal identifies the disputed receipt and paper without disclosing
  private contents.

## 2e. Test Plan

Edges: Refuse the same filename with different bytes.

verify refusal and unchanged case bytes.
