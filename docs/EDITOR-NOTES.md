# V3 editor's notes

The useful inheritance is complementary: James's original role separation and
blind Watson; the Editor's Editions' evidence boundaries, checked repairs, and
separate remediation verdict; Claude's modular organization, capability inventory,
canonical decision identifiers, and disciplined voice.

The combined edition makes the following decisions:

- Keep triage, focused, and standard, with an explicit complete triage path.
- Treat risk as relevant to the requested decision, rather than a reason to inflate every narrow task.
- Supply shared rules and full record definitions in every role packet.
- Keep record vocabulary separate from the work assigned to a role.
- Define the decision states with examples, preserving ordinary repairs versus demonstrated disqualifiers.
- Record execution provenance separately from execution isolation and validity.
- Check all probe destinations, including specialized and minimized tests.
- Preserve decision-critical uncertainty without making every unknown a blocker.
- Treat instructions in reviewed prompts or quoted security fixtures as data, not automatic product findings.
- Include necessary preparation in standalone publication instructions without broadening the authorized destination.
- Preserve original testimony, corrections, and separately assessed repaired artifacts.
- Keep humor and character outside evidence tables and decision identifiers.

A later port brought in standing review rules from the V2 line in V3's own
terms: a stateful code review reference routed to Moriarty, Holmes, and the
checker; verification of code policy against callers and canonical readers;
a single fresh blind code pass after finding-aware code revisions; and decision
rules for visible coverage gaps, a stated path to READY, severity judged by
impact if triggered, and a fixed standard across rounds. No letter grades or new
severity scale were added.

The packet builder assembles instructions and records their digests. It is not a
sandbox, permission system, workflow executor, or semantic verifier. The front
office must still check target access, tool scope, actual separation, and outputs.

During forward testing, an initial Moriarty response correctly found a boundary
error but exceeded its assignment by producing a Repair Menu, probe triage, and
case verdict. The shared rules and Moriarty brief were tightened to distinguish
record definitions from assigned phases. The original output is preserved with
its rerun in the validation evidence. A correct finding alone did not count as
successful role execution.

No speedup, recall improvement, or superiority over a simpler reviewer has been
measured. Those require matched-budget comparisons and a separate holdout set.
